Journal of Neuroscience Methods submission package
=================================================

Upload these to the journal:

cover_letter.docx   letter to the editor
manuscript.docx     main text with tables and figure captions; Times New
                    Roman 12pt, double-spaced, line and page numbers
highlights.docx     required separate file: 5 points, each under 85 characters
supplementary.docx  Supplementary Material: Sections S1-S6, Tables S1-S7,
                    with Figures S1-S4 embedded
Figure1.tif         design of the comparison
Figure2.tif         agreement with the estimator matched, Stanford HARDI
FigureS1-S4.tif     supplementary figures, also as separate files
                    (S1 interface, S2-S3 brain masks, S4 Sherbrooke agreement)

All figures: TIFF LZW, RGB, 300 dpi. Figure1, Figure2 and FigureS2-S4 are
2126 px wide (180 mm). FigureS1 is a screenshot of the interface and is
1456 px wide (123 mm).

Tables and figure captions sit at the end of manuscript.docx, which is the
convention; the typesetter places each one at its first mention in the text.

This README is for your own reference. The journal does not need it.

Source: article_jnm.md, supplementary_jnm.md, highlights_jnm.md,
cover_letter_jnm.md. Rebuild with: bash scripts/build_submission.sh
